Credit Limit Policy (TestCard Servicing Guidelines, Synthetic)

Customers may request their current credit limit and available credit; this may be provided directly
by a frontline agent or automated system.
Customers may request a credit limit increase once every six months. Requests may be evaluated
automatically using existing account data if the customer has at least twelve months of on-time
payment history; otherwise the request must be escalated for manual underwriting review.
A credit limit decrease initiated by the company must always be communicated with the required
adverse action notice and is handled by the Risk team, not frontline servicing.
