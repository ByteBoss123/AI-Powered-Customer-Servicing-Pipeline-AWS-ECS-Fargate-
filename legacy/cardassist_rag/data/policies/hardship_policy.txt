Hardship Program Policy (TestCard Servicing Guidelines, Synthetic)

Customers experiencing documented financial hardship (job loss, medical emergency, natural disaster)
may request enrollment in a temporary hardship program offering reduced minimum payments and a
paused APR increase for up to six months.
Enrollment always requires escalation to the Hardship team for verification of documentation and
is never approved automatically or by a frontline agent alone.
Customers already enrolled in a hardship program are not eligible for the standard late fee waiver
described in the Late Fee Policy; hardship terms take precedence.
