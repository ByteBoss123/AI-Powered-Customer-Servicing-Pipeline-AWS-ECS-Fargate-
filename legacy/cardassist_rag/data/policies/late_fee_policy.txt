Late Fee Policy (TestCard Servicing Guidelines, Synthetic)

A late fee of $29 is applied when a minimum payment is not received by the due date.
A late fee of $40 is applied for a second late payment within the same six-billing-cycle window.
Accounts in good standing (no late payment in the prior twelve billing cycles) are eligible for one
courtesy late fee waiver per rolling twelve-month period. This waiver may only be granted once per
twelve months, regardless of how many late payments occur afterward.
A second waiver request within the same twelve-month period must be escalated to a servicing
supervisor for manual review; frontline agents and automated systems are not authorized to approve it.
Customers who are enrolled in a hardship program are evaluated under the Hardship Program Policy
instead of this policy.
