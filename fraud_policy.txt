Fraud Claims Policy (TestCard Servicing Guidelines, Synthetic)

Any report of a lost or stolen card, or any suspected unauthorized transaction, must be escalated
immediately to the Fraud team. Frontline agents and automated systems may not resolve fraud claims
directly, but may confirm the card has been temporarily locked and a replacement has been ordered.
Customers are not liable for confirmed unauthorized transactions once a fraud claim is filed.
Any request involving a customer's Social Security number, full account number, or identity verification
document must be escalated and is never handled by an automated system.
