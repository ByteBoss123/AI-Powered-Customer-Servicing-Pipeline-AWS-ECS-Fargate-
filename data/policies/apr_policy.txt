APR and Rate Change Policy (TestCard Servicing Guidelines, Synthetic)

Standard variable APR is tied to the Prime Rate plus a margin disclosed in the cardholder agreement.
Customers may request their current APR and the date of the last rate change; this information may be
provided directly by a frontline agent or automated system.
A penalty APR may be applied after a payment is 60 days past due, per the cardholder agreement.
Any request to have a penalty APR removed or to negotiate a permanent rate reduction must be
escalated to a retention specialist and is not authorized for automated resolution.
