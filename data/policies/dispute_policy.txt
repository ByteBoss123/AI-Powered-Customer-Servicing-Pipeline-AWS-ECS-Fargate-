Transaction Dispute Policy (TestCard Servicing Guidelines, Synthetic)

Customers may dispute a transaction within 60 calendar days of the statement date on which it appeared.
Disputes involving amounts under $50 with a clear duplicate-charge pattern may be provisionally credited
by a frontline agent while the investigation is pending.
Disputes involving amounts of $50 or more, or any dispute alleging unauthorized use or identity theft,
must be escalated to the Fraud and Disputes team and are not authorized for frontline resolution.
Provisional credit is reversed if the investigation determines the transaction was valid.
Customers must be informed that investigations can take up to 90 days under applicable regulations.
